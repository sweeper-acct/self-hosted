AI Workforce
What is Sweeper?

Sweeper is a Professional Work Operating System built specifically for Australian accounting firms. It uses AI to handle the repetitive, high-volume steps in BAS, GST, payroll, tax, and SMSF workflows — while keeping a human accountant in control of every decision that matters.

It is designed for firms with one or more partner-led teams, each running independent client portfolios.

The AI handles the mechanical parts of accounting work: reading bank statements, classifying transactions, coding GST, and drafting BAS reports. Think of it as a tireless junior that processes the data and flags anything uncertain.

Every AI output passes through a human validation gate before it can advance. The AI never submits, certifies, or archives anything on its own.

No. The AI proposes — humans decide. Every transaction classification, GST code, and BAS figure can be reviewed and overridden by your team before anything is certified.

Rows the AI is uncertain about are explicitly flagged as Review Required. The workflow cannot advance past a review step until a qualified team member approves.

All data is stored in AWS Sydney (ap-southeast-2) — it never leaves Australia. Sweeper is compliant with the Privacy Act 1988.

Each firm's data is isolated at the database level using Row-Level Security (RLS). One firm can never read or write another firm's data, even within the same platform.

BAS / GST Module
Bank statement extraction and GST coding

The BAS / GST Module handles the full end-to-end BAS preparation workflow: bank statement extraction, transaction-level GST classification, workpaper review, client confirmation, and partner certification — including optional push to Xero or QuickBooks.

It covers monthly, quarterly, and annual BAS cycles for cash and accruals basis clients.

One bank statement read = one PDF or spreadsheet file processed through extraction. A client with two bank accounts in a quarter = two reads.

If extraction is re-run on the same file (e.g. after you delete and re-upload), it counts as an additional read. Top-up packs are available if you exceed your monthly quota.

GST coding follows a three-priority system:

1. Custom rules — rules your firm defines for specific clients (e.g. "Bunnings → Taxable Supplies"). Absolute authority, never overridden by AI.

2. Standard rules — built-in ATO classification rules applied deterministically.

3. AI inference — when no rule matches, the AI classifies the transaction and flags it as Review Required. A qualified team member must confirm or override before the workpaper can advance.

Human validation gate
Why humans stay in control

Every AI-generated output — bank statement extraction, GST coding, BAS draft — pauses at a human checkpoint before it can advance. The workflow does not proceed until a qualified team member reviews and submits or approves.

This is a structural rule, not a preference setting. It cannot be bypassed or disabled.

The approval chain is configured per team and follows your firm's sign-off structure:

Junior validates the extraction and the GST workpaper. Senior reviews the GST workpaper (if enabled). Manager approves the BAS draft (if enabled). Partner confirms with the client and certifies for lodgement.

Steps can be enabled or disabled per team. The Junior validation and Partner certification steps are always required.

Working paper suite
Structured workpapers for every BAS step

The suite includes a structured GST workpaper with four tabs: All Transactions, Ready for BAS, Non-GST, and Review Required. Each tab shows only the rows relevant to that category, with sortable columns and inline editing.

A separate BAS Summary workpaper shows the calculated G-field report (G1 through 8A) that feeds into the BAS lodgement. Senior and Manager review pages display the same data from their perspective in the approval chain.

Yes. Any team member with review access can override GST codes, amounts, and status directly in the workpaper. Every edit is recorded in the immutable case log with the editor's name and timestamp.

Overrides by a human always take precedence over the AI's original classification.

Client query portal
Ask clients about specific transactions

During the GST workpaper review, your team can select one or more Review Required transactions and send a secure query to the client. The client receives a link — no login or account needed.

They see the transaction details (date, merchant, amount) alongside your question, answer in plain text, and can attach receipts or invoices directly from their phone. The response lands immediately in the workpaper's Client Reply column.

Yes. Select multiple transactions in the workpaper and send them as a single batch. The client answers all questions on one page. You can track which batches are pending, answered, or resolved from the case detail screen — and revoke an unanswered batch if needed.

Magic link client access
Secure access without accounts

A magic link is a time-limited, password-protected URL your team generates for a specific client task. The client clicks the link, enters a simple password (set by you), and accesses only the specific page they need — a query form or a BAS confirmation document.

Links expire after 7 days. Clients never create an account, and they can only see the specific content the link was generated for.

Client confirmation node
Formalising client sign-off

Before the Partner certifies the BAS, the Manager sends the client a BAS Confirmation PDF via magic link. The client reviews the BAS figures, signs (or initials) a digital copy, and uploads it back through the same link.

The signed document is stored in the case archive automatically. The workflow advances only after the signed document is received — it cannot be skipped.

Structured data · Excel export
Workpaper exports for your records

The GST workpaper exports as a formatted multi-sheet Excel file: All Transactions, Ready for BAS, Non-GST, and Review Required — matching the four tabs in the on-screen workpaper.

The BAS Summary exports separately with the full G-field report (G1 through 8A), client details, ABN, period, and GST basis. Both files are available from the case detail screen.

Yes. Any team member with access to a case can download the Excel workpaper from the case detail screen. The raw CSV is also available for any team member who needs it for further analysis.

Xero / QuickBooks push
Post BAS journals to your accounting software

Sweeper connects to Xero and QuickBooks Online via official OAuth integrations. Each firm connects one accounting software. The connection is configured once in Team Settings by the firm owner or admin.

After a Partner certifies the BAS, Sweeper posts a manual journal to the connected software with all BAS G-field amounts: G1 (total sales), G11 (non-capital purchases), 1A (GST on sales), 1B (GST on purchases), and 8A (net GST position).

The push can be automatic on certify (configured per client) or triggered manually. It is always the Partner or Manager who initiates — never automated without human confirmation.

Isolated partner workspaces
Multi-team structure with data isolation

Each partner in your firm leads their own team. Each team has its own clients, approval chain, and SLA settings. A team member can only see the clients and cases assigned to their team — there is no cross-team data visibility.

Isolation is enforced at the database level using Row-Level Security (RLS), not just the application layer. Even if two partners share the same Sweeper subscription, their data is structurally separated.

No. You can create as many partner teams as your firm needs within the team member limit for your plan. A 10-partner firm can have 10 isolated workspaces on a single subscription.
