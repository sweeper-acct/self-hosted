Data & Structure

Your firm's data never leaves your control

From how your teams are organised to where your AI calls go — Sweeper is designed around firm ownership of every layer.

Team structure

Multiple partner teams, one platform

Each partner runs their own team with independent approval chains, client allocations and SLA profiles. Teams are isolated by default — a Partner sees their own team's work; firm-wide oversight is available to administrators without crossing team boundaries.

Unlimited teams per firm
AI isolation

Every team member has their own AI workspace

Each staff member's AI view is scoped to their role and assigned cases. Row-level security enforced at the database layer means it is structurally impossible for one team's client data to be read by another — not a policy, a technical guarantee.

RLS enforced at DB layer
Data sovereignty

Use your own Anthropic API key

Firms with an existing Anthropic enterprise agreement can supply their own API key. All AI calls flow through your firm's account — under your data terms, in your usage records, with no AI traffic routed through Sweeper's infrastructure.

Encrypted at rest · never via API
Why Sweeper

Built specifically for Australian accounting compliance

AI-assisted, human-certified

No AI output auto-advances. Every step requires an authorised professional to review, correct, and approve before the workflow moves on. AI does not determine professional conclusions — your team does.

Role-aware workflow

Junior → Senior → Manager → Partner. Each role sees only their queue. Approval chains are fully configurable per team.

ATO-compliant audit trail

Tamper-resistant case log records every action — who approved, what they saw, when. Five-year retention, ready for ATO review.

Australian data sovereignty

Client data held in Australia (Sydney, ap-southeast-2). Your workpapers and client data remain your firm's property — Sweeper processes data to deliver the service, never to claim ownership. Designed to support compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Xero and QuickBooks sync

Certify once — BAS journals post to your accounting software automatically. G1 through 8A mapped to your chart of accounts. Zero re-keying.

Client queries by magic link

Flag a transaction, send a secure link. No portal, no login. Client answers and attaches receipts from any device — evidence goes straight into the workpaper.
